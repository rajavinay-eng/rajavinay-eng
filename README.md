## Projects

### [H&M fashion recommendations + AI product search](https://github.com/rajavinay-eng/hm-fashion-recommendations)
Predicting the 12 products each customer buys next week, using H&M's Kaggle data (31.8M purchases; I modelled a 20% customer sample). My LightGBM ranker scored 3.5x the popularity baseline and 14% above my best hand-made rule, checked with a paired bootstrap. A two-tower PyTorch model didn't win, and I explain why. There's also an AI search where Gemini answers only from retrieved products: 16 out of 16 test answers stayed grounded.
`Python` `DuckDB` `LightGBM` `PyTorch` `sentence-transformers` `Gemini` `Streamlit`
